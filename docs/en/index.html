Privacy policy — SaluteInOrdine

Last updated: 18/11/2026  ·  Version: draft 0.3 (SaluteInOrdine app 1.0.0)
This is a translation. The reference text is the Italian version: if the versions differ, the Italian version prevails.
In short.
  • Your health data and your family's stay on your phone only. We do not send them to any server, neither to us nor to anyone else. We cannot see them and, if you lose them, we cannot recover them.
  • No sign-up and no account. No advertising, no usage statistics, no automatic crash reports, no artificial intelligence.
  • Reading text from photos happens entirely on the phone: photos and text go to no one (section 3.5).
  • The "Call for help" button calls the numbers you choose, in the order you decide. The numbers stay on the phone. It is not an emergency service: in an emergency, call 112 or your country's emergency number (section 3.8).
  • You can create several profiles (for example for your children or for a parent you care for). Profiles of minors are managed by a parent or guardian.
  • Data leave the phone only when you create a file (encrypted backup, PDF or CSV) and save or share it, or when you make a call.
  • The app connects to the internet only to activate Premium and to check for and download updates (sections 4 and 5). These connections contain no health data. [TO BE VERIFIED with network traffic analysis on the final APK]
  • SaluteInOrdine is an archive and a diary. It does not replace your doctor, your country's electronic health record or emergency services (section 13).

3. The data you enter in the app

3.1 Country

At first launch you choose your country. It is used only to show you the right fields and your country's emergency number: for Italy, for example, tax code, health card, local health authority (ASL), prescription-charge exemptions and references to the Italian electronic health record (FSE); for other countries, generic fields. The choice stays on the phone, you can change it in the settings and it is not sent to anyone. The country and the language used to read text from photos are saved in the app's preferences on the phone; the list of emergency numbers is built into the app. If you choose a country other than Italy, the tax code and exemptions already entered stay saved but are not shown.

3.2 What you can save

For each profile you can save, if you wish, the following information. Almost all fields are optional.

  • profile: name, date of birth, whether the person is a minor, photo, sex, spoken language, parent or guardian of reference;
  • administrative data: in Italy tax code, health card number and expiry, ASL or Region; in other countries the generic fields provided by the app (see section 3.3);
  • prescription-charge exemptions (Italy only): code as written on the document, description, expiry;
  • contacts: GP or paediatrician, pharmacy, specialists, emergency contact with relationship (for example "daughter");
  • allergies and intolerances, with the reaction described by you;
  • conditions: current or past illnesses, including disabilities, missing organs and transplants;
  • medicines: name, reminder times you choose, free notes, and the intake log ("taken" or "skipped");
  • blood group, with the report you took it from, if any;
  • vaccinations, with date and attached certificate;
  • surgeries and hospital stays, with any discharge letter;
  • devices and implants (for example pacemaker, prosthesis, hearing aid);
  • visits and tests, with reports, photos and attached documents and the questions to ask the doctor;
  • "My fields": fields you create yourself, with any name and content. For each one you choose whether to show it on the doctor's sheet or on the emergency sheet; by default it appears on neither. "My fields" always appear in the profile's full PDF and in the CSV, and never in notifications.

Most of these are data concerning health, which the GDPR treats as special categories of data (Art. 4(15) and Art. 9). What you write in "My fields" may be too: you decide what to write there. Everything stays only in the app's private storage on your phone, in an encrypted database; attachments are encrypted too (details in section 12).

They are not transmitted to anyone. We have no server that receives them, we cannot read them and we cannot recover them if you lose them.

3.3 Tax code, health card and identification numbers

They are optional. In the app they are fully hidden: you see them only in the profile edit form, by tapping the eye icon, and they do not appear on other screens. They are not included in the PDF and CSV files you export unless you choose so when exporting. They never appear on the emergency sheet or in notifications and are never used to activate Premium. They are, however, included in the encrypted backup (section 3.10), which is used to restore all your data. The same applies to the generic identification field used outside Italy.

3.4 Photos and documents

You can attach photos, PDFs and other documents by choosing them with Android's file or photo picker, which gives the app only the file you choose. To take a photo the app opens the phone's camera app, which returns only the photo taken: SaluteInOrdine does not ask for camera permission or for access to the phone's storage. Files are copied into the app's private storage, encrypted and saved with neutral names; they do not leave the phone unless you export or share them.

3.5 Reading text from photos

If you wish, the app can read the text of a photographed report or certificate to pre-fill fields. The text read is always shown to you: you check and confirm it before it is saved. The app does not interpret the content, does not extract values and does not judge them. Reading can make mistakes.

Reading uses Tesseract, an open-source text recognition engine that works entirely on the phone. The photo and the text read are not sent to anyone, neither to us nor to third parties, and reading sends no technical data to any external service.

The Tesseract language models (Italian, English, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese) are bundled with the app: they are not downloaded from the internet. On first use they are copied into the app's private storage, which is excluded from backups. While reading, the app does not use the network. The component licences (tesseract_ocr, BSD-3-Clause; Tesseract4Android, Apache-2.0) are in Information › Licences.

3.6 Reminders and notifications

Reminders for medicines, visits, vaccinations and expiry dates (for example health card or exemption) are set by you: day, time and text. The app does not suggest times or quantities. Notifications are scheduled and shown by the phone: they do not go through any server, neither ours nor Google's.

To avoid showing health data to anyone looking at your phone, by default notifications only say "SaluteInOrdine – You have a reminder". In the settings you can choose to also show the name of the medicine (also for prescription renewal), visit or vaccine; for exemptions only "Exemption expiring" appears, without the code; the profile name, tax code and health conditions never appear. Notifications are marked as private: on the lock screen Android can hide their content, depending on your phone settings.

The "taken" or "skipped" log is a diary you fill in: the app does not change times or reminders based on what you record.

3.7 Emergency sheet and doctor's sheet

With the data you entered, the app builds an emergency sheet (for example blood group, allergies, important conditions, medicines you marked, devices, emergency contact, doctor, "My fields" you chose to show) and a more complete doctor's sheet. You see them in the app; with Premium you can save them as PDF.

Anyone who sees the sheet, or the PDF, can read the data it contains. You choose which information to include. A PDF that is printed or saved in another app is no longer protected by the SaluteInOrdine lock.

SaluteInOrdine does not show health data on the phone's lock screen. The app can explain how to copy some data into Android's emergency information: this is an Android feature, not ours. Google warns that anyone who picks up your phone can see that information even when the phone is locked, and that you can turn it off in the Personal Safety app (Google guide).

3.8 "Call for help"

"Call for help" is a button to quickly call the people you choose: for example a family member, your doctor or your country's emergency number. At the start the app asks you to enter these numbers as a first step, and reminds you until you do.

  • You decide the numbers and their order. You can put first a family member who lives nearby and then the emergency number, or the other way round.
  • Numbers and names stay only on the phone, in the app's encrypted database. We do not send them to anyone and do not receive them. You can type the numbers or pick them with Android's contact picker: the app receives only the name and number of the contact you choose and has no permission to read your contacts.
  • 5-second notice. When you tap "Call for help", the app shows for 5 seconds who it is about to call, with a Cancel button; then the call starts by itself.
  • Call permission. To start the call by itself the app needs the Android permission "Phone – make and manage phone calls" (CALL_PHONE). The app asks for it only once, after an explanation, when you save the first person. If you do not grant it, the app opens the phone's dialer with the number already typed and you start the call.
  • Emergency numbers. Android does not allow apps to start a call to emergency numbers by themselves (for example 112): for these numbers the app opens the dialer with the number already typed and you tap the call button.
  • Call the next one. After a call you can move on to the next person in the list, with a new 5-second notice. If the list is empty, "Call for help" offers the emergency number of the country you chose.
  • Always reachable, even without PIN. "Call for help" opens above the PIN screen and from the menu that appears when you long-press the app icon. This is intentional: in an emergency the button must not be locked. It means, however, that anyone holding your phone, even with the app locked, can see the names of your help contacts and start a call. When the app is locked, only the names and your country's emergency number are visible, not the other numbers; the rest of the data stays protected by the PIN. Only you can start "Call for help", from the app or from the icon menu: other apps installed on the phone cannot start the call through SaluteInOrdine. [TO BE CONFIRMED: developer change in progress]
  • The call is a normal phone call, handled by your phone and your carrier, at the cost of your plan. The person receiving it sees your phone number, as in any call (unless you have hidden your number in the phone settings). SaluteInOrdine does not record the call, does not send text messages, does not send your location or health data and does not keep a list of calls made.
  • No fall detection. The app does not detect falls or illness and does not call anyone by itself: the call starts only if you tap the button.

The app can also explain how to turn on Android's Emergency SOS feature: this is an Android feature, not ours, and follows the rules of Android and of the phone manufacturer.

"Call for help" is not an emergency service and does not guarantee that anyone answers: the call may fail, for example without signal, without credit or with the phone off. In an emergency, call 112 or your country's emergency number.

The numbers you enter belong to other people (family members, doctor): enter them only if they agree to be called when needed.

3.9 Export and sharing (Premium)

With Premium you can export data as PDF or CSV. The file is created on the phone and then you save or share it, with the app you choose (email, WhatsApp, Drive, printing). The file does not go through our servers.

PDF and CSV files are not encrypted and have no password. The tax code and health card number are included only if you tick the box when exporting. Once they leave the app, they are handled by whoever receives them or by the service where you save them, under its own rules. Share them only with people you trust, for example your doctor. The app offers no shared access: the only way to show data to someone is a file you create.

3.10 Encrypted backup

From the settings you can create an encrypted backup file with a password you choose. The password is not saved or communicated to anyone. The file is encrypted with AES-256-GCM; the key is derived from the password with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (600,000 iterations). You decide where to save it. We do not receive it and cannot open it.

The backup contains all data of all profiles, including tax code, health card, "My fields", the "Call for help" numbers and all attachments (photos and documents). It does not contain the Premium licence, the PIN or the app settings. Restoring replaces the data on the phone with those in the backup. If you restore a backup made with an earlier version of the app, without "Call for help", the help numbers already on the phone are kept.

If you forget the password, the backup cannot be opened, not even by us.

To keep health data out of the cloud, the app excludes its data from Android's automatic backup to Google Drive and from phone-to-phone transfer: no SaluteInOrdine data end up in your Google account backup. If you change phone, you take your data with your backup file.

3.11 Data of family members and minors

You can create profiles for other people, for example your children or an elderly parent you care for. These are health data of other people: enter them only if you look after their health.

  • Minors: their profiles are created and managed by a parent or guardian, on their own phone. The app does not ask minors to sign up and does not collect data from them.
  • Other adults: we recommend doing it with their agreement and showing them what you saved. If they ask, correct or delete their data.
  • Family members' data, like yours, stay only on your phone and are never used to activate Premium.

3.12 App lock

At first launch the app offers to protect it with a PIN (4 to 8 digits) and, if you wish, with the fingerprint or face recognised by the phone. It is not mandatory. The PIN is not stored in clear: the app keeps only a hash of it (PBKDF2). Fingerprint or face recognition is done by Android: the app receives only the result.

The PIN protects access to the app, it does not encrypt the data: database and attachment encryption is always on, with keys kept by the phone (section 12), even if you do not set a PIN. When the app goes to the background it locks after the time you choose (1 minute by default; you can choose "immediately" or 5 minutes). "Call for help" remains reachable even when the app is locked (section 3.8).

If you forget the PIN we cannot recover it: you can unlock with fingerprint or face, if enabled, or use "Delete all data" and restore a backup.

By default the app blocks screenshots and hides its content in the recent apps list on all screens. You can turn this protection off in the settings.

4. Activating Premium on the phone

A Premium key is valid on 2 phones at most. To enforce this limit, when you activate the key the app connects to our licence server (licenze.hubertmontero8514.workers.dev, a Cloudflare Workers service run by us) and sends:

  • the app name (saluteinordine);
  • the licence key, which contains the buyer's email address, the plan and the expiry date;
  • a phone fingerprint: a code derived with a hash function (SHA-256) from the app name and the phone's Android identifier (ANDROID_ID), then shortened to 22 characters. The Android identifier is never sent in clear and the fingerprint is different for each app, so it cannot be used to link your use of different apps.

These requests contain no profile, health or "Call for help" data. The same request is repeated automatically when the activation is about to expire (less than 20 days) and only if the phone is online; it is also made when you tap Deactivate on this phone. There are no other requests to the licence server. The server replies with a signed activation that the app checks on the phone, even offline.

The licence server does not store your email or the key in clear. It stores only: the SHA-256 hash of the key; the phone fingerprint; the plan type; the activation date and the last renewal date; if you change phone, the date of the last automatic replacement (one every 30 days); if a key is blocked (for example after a refund or abuse), the hash of that key. The Deactivate on this phone button, on the Premium screen, deletes from the server the row linking the key to that phone.

The Android identifier is read only to activate Premium, never for advertising, statistics or profiling.

12. Security

  • Encrypted database: profile data, "My fields" and "Call for help" numbers are in a SQLite database encrypted with ChaCha20-Poly1305 (SQLite3MultipleCiphers). The key is a random 32-byte sequence generated on the phone and protected with the Android Keystore. Deleted data are overwritten in the database (secure delete).
  • Encrypted attachments: photos and documents are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, with a separate key protected with the Android Keystore, and saved with neutral names.
  • Encrypted backup: AES-256-GCM with a key derived from your password (section 3.10).
  • App lock with PIN or biometrics and screenshot protection on by default (section 3.12).
  • No automatic backup of data to Google and no automatic phone-to-phone transfer (section 3.10).
  • Communication with the licence server and for updates uses HTTPS.

No measure is infallible: anyone who knows the PIN or unlocks the phone can see the data in the app, and anyone holding the phone can use "Call for help". We recommend keeping the screen lock on, updating the operating system and making regular backups: if you lose or reset the phone without a backup, the data cannot be recovered.

13. The app does not replace your doctor, electronic health records or emergency services

SaluteInOrdine is an archive and a diary of the health data you enter. It does not diagnose, does not give medical advice, does not calculate or suggest doses, does not check drug interactions, does not flag abnormal values and does not interpret reports.

You enter the data: the app does not check that they are complete or correct. It is not an electronic health record or a health service of your country and does not replace them; for official documents, what is issued by health facilities applies.

"Call for help" is not an emergency service. For any health concern, contact your doctor or pharmacist. In an emergency, call 112 or your country's emergency number.
